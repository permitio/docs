## What are Permit Elements?

**[Permit Elements](/embeddable-uis/overview)** are embeddable UI
components for access management. They let your users handle **[access
requests](/embeddable-uis/element/access-request)** and approvals inside your app, while your policy in Permit decides who can do what.

You add permission management to your app without building the UI yourself.

## Configuring your JWKS

Configure **[JSON Web Key Sets](/embeddable-uis/embedding-elements#configuring-jwks-for-your-environment)** (JWKS)
for your environment. A JWKS is a set of public keys used to validate JSON Web Tokens (JWTs), confirming that each token
is legitimate and comes from a trusted issuer.

With JWKS configured, the public keys needed for token verification are fetched automatically,
so only properly authenticated users can log in to the elements.

## Building the Authorization Policy

Decide what your users can do in your app: **create resources, define roles, and specify the actions** users can take on those resources.
Then **assign the roles** to your users, so each user gets the access they need.

## Embedding the Elements in our App

**Embed the Permit Elements** in your app. First, add
the generated **[iframe](/embeddable-uis/element/access-request#creating-an-iframe)** snippet
to your app's codebase.

Then log users in to the element, so it acts on behalf of the signed-in user.
