View how many projects, environments, users, tenants, and monthly active users (MAUs) your Permit.io workspace uses, so you can compare usage against the quotas of your Permit plan. This page is for workspace owners and admins who track usage and billing.

## Workspace Level Usage

To view the usage of your workspace:

1. In the Permit dashboard sidebar, open the **Projects** screen.
2. Find the workspace usage in the top right corner of the **Projects** screen.
3. To see the usage history of tenants and MAUs in your workspace, click the **(?)** icon next to the usage.

  <source src={orgUsage} type="video/mp4" />
</video>

The workspace usage shows the number of:

- **Projects** in your workspace
- **Environments** in your workspace
- **Users** in your workspace
- **Tenants** in your workspace
- **Monthly active users** (MAUs) in your workspace

## Environment Level Usage

To view the usage of one environment, open the **Projects** screen in the Permit dashboard sidebar. Each environment card shows the number of:

- **Tenants** in the environment
- **Users** in the environment
- **Monthly active users** (MAUs) in the environment
- **Resources** in the environment
- **Roles** in the environment
- **Members** with access to the environment

![Environment card on the Projects screen showing the tenant, user, MAU, resource, role, and member counts of the environment](/ui-videos/usage/usage-env.png)

## MAUs & Tenants Usage

Permit plans set quotas on **monthly active users** (MAUs) and **tenants**, among other limits.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Monthly active users (MAUs) | The unique users of your application that you check permissions for in a month |
| Tenants | Isolated units within an environment, each with its own users, data, and permissions. In a multi-tenant application, a tenant usually represents one of your customers. See [Tenants](/how-to/build-policies/policy-basics#tenants). |

:::tip Plan quotas and billing
For the MAU and tenant quotas of each plan and how Permit bills usage, see the [Permit.io pricing page](https://www.permit.io/pricing).
:::
