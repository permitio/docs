Check whether Permit.io is already building a capability before you plan work around its absence. This page explains what the Permit.io roadmap shows and how it relates to the feature requests board. It is for developers and technical decision makers who evaluate Permit or schedule work that depends on a capability.

## What the Productlane roadmap shows

The Permit.io roadmap runs on Productlane. It groups ideas into two statuses, **In Progress** and **Completed**, and shows a vote count next to each idea. Open an idea to read its description, or choose **New request** to submit one. The Permit team reviews each submitted request and links it to an idea on the roadmap, so the request appears in the list and you can follow its status.

Open the [Permit.io roadmap on Productlane](https://permit.productlane.com/roadmap).

:::info The roadmap lists a small set of ideas, not every planned change
The Productlane roadmap holds only the ideas the Permit team has published there. An absent idea does not mean Permit has rejected it. The [Canny feature requests board](https://permitio.canny.io/feature-requests) holds the larger public backlog, including requests that are under review. Check both boards before you conclude that a capability is unplanned, and email [support@permit.io](mailto:support@permit.io) when you need a commitment for a specific capability.
:::

## How the roadmap, the boards, and the changelog fit together

| Question | Source |
|---|---|
| Is Permit building this now, or has it finished it? | The [Productlane roadmap](https://permit.productlane.com/roadmap), statuses **In Progress** and **Completed** |
| Has someone already asked for this, and can I vote for it? | The [Canny feature requests board](https://permitio.canny.io/feature-requests), with the statuses under review, planned, in progress, and complete. See [Request a feature](/updates-and-feedback/feature-requests). |
| When did this ship, and in which version? | The SDK, PDP, and CLI release notes. See [Changelog](/updates-and-feedback/changelog). |
