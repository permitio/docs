The Permit.io roadmap shows what the Permit team is working on and what it has completed. Check it before you plan work around a capability Permit doesn't have yet.

## What the roadmap contains

The roadmap page on Productlane groups ideas by status, such as **In Progress** and **Completed**. You can open an idea to read its details, and submit an idea from the same page. The team reviews each submitted request and links it to an idea on the roadmap, so you can follow its status.

## Open the roadmap

Open the [Permit.io roadmap and feature requests on Productlane](https://permit.productlane.com/roadmap).
